## Public report contract

```swift
public func stats(
    from: DayKey? = nil,
    through: DayKey? = nil,
    now: Date = Date(),
    calendar: Calendar = .current
) throws -> StatsReport
```

Omitting both dates returns 28 inclusive Gregorian civil dates ending today in the supplied time zone.
Supplying only `through` returns 28 dates ending on that date. Supplying only `from` ends today.
Invalid, reversed, greater-than-366-day, and future-ending ranges throw `ThreeError.invalid` before a database read.
The read writes nothing and posts no change signal.

`StatsReport` is Codable, Hashable, and Sendable.
It exposes bounds, read time, time zone, partial status, provenance, missing-data flags, aggregate metrics, daily values, Monday-based weeks, per-tag results, and the streak.
Each day includes the available commitment capture time and frozen close time.
The report uses bounded dates, rather than claiming knowledge of the entire history.

Ratios expose `numerator`, `denominator`, `value`, and `percent` in the encoded result.
A zero denominator encodes null `value` and `percent`. A positive denominator with no matching recorded numerator returns zero.
Minutes, percentages, averages, and rates are calculated in Core. SwiftUI formats these returned values.

## Metric definitions

| Metric | Numerator and denominator | Unknown or partial evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily focus | Sum of `HistorySession.actualSeconds` for focus sessions starting on that civil date. Breaks are excluded. | Today is partial. Historical coverage is unknown. Zero means no matching recorded focus. |
| Seven-day average | Focus minutes over seven included civil dates divided by seven, including recorded zero days. | The first six values are null. `averageDayCount` exposes the available prefix. No outside-range activity is inferred. |
| Weekly completion | Done original commitment IDs divided by all original commitment IDs from nondeleted committed plans with snapshots. Later additions do not enter the denominator. Later drops, deleted tasks, and carries do not remove original commitments. | Drafts and legacy committed plans without snapshots are excluded. Counts expose included snapshots and excluded legacy plans. Current week and incomplete boundary weeks are partial. |
| Actual to planned | Recorded focus on dates with prospective commitment snapshots divided by the original captured planned seconds on those dates. Unplanned focus on those dates still counts. | Draft and legacy dates are excluded from both sides. Ratios can exceed 100 percent. Current-row planned seconds remain separately labelled derived. |
| Focused-rated share | Focused ratings divided by all recorded focus-session ratings, globally and by current category. | Missing ratings remain separate. No unrated session becomes scattered. Missing references retain their recorded focus in neutral category buckets. |
| Block hits and unblocks per focus hour | Recorded events linked to the selected focus sessions divided by those sessions' actual focus hours. | No focus hours means null rates. Unassociated events are reported separately and never enter these rates. |

Completion uses frozen close task outcomes when a `dayClose` record exists.
For open committed plans it uses current nondeleted task outcomes.
It does not infer a historical task status timeline.
Frozen close durations are not reused for actual time. All actual time continues through History and `Session.elapsed` so later recovery adjustments have one owner.

Current-row derived planned time follows History's contract.
It excludes dropped, carried, and deleted tasks. It is context, not a replacement for missing commitment snapshots.
The v11 tests prove that editing a block, adding later work, carrying a source, or deleting a committed reference preserves the original commitment denominator.

### Block attribution and coverage

A recorded event with a matching focus-session ID follows that session's start date, even across midnight.
The query includes linked events for selected sessions and excludes events linked to focus sessions starting outside the requested dates.
Events with no session ID, a missing session, or a break-session association follow their own occurrence date.
Those events contribute to separate unassociated counts. A missing non-null session reference also increments the unresolved-session count.

Top targets contain at most three stored rules across sites and apps, sorted by recorded hit count, then unblock count, then stable rule ID.
Their counts expose associated and unassociated events separately. A missing rule retains its ID with an unavailable target.
Reasons, URLs, page titles, and synthetic browser activity are not returned.

Hits are the recorded deduplicated block events. Unblocks are granted exceptions, not requests or measured browsing time.
Zero events cannot prove that the user avoided distraction or that every app or browser was covered.
The report and view explicitly state that unconfigured rules, unsupported browsers, and other unrecorded activity are not measured.

### Tags and break choices

Every matching tag receives a session's full focus time. Global focus counts the session once.
Completion and committed planned minutes use original task IDs with current task-tag membership.
Historical tag membership was not captured and is not reconstructed.
Missing committed-task references are flagged because their former tag membership is unavailable.
Unassociated block events do not acquire tags.

`StatsBreaks` reports ended focus sessions, pending focus sessions, recorded decisions, taken choices, skipped choices, and missing choices.
Skipped share divides recorded skips by recorded decisions only.
A running or paused focus session is pending. A finished focus session without a decision is unknown.
Pre-v11 sessions, generic stops, and missing decisions are never converted to zero skips or inferred taken breaks.

### Streak definition

A qualifying civil date needs a nondeleted committed plan and at least one currently done, nondeleted task attached to that plan.
The task's stored `doneAt`, converted in the report time zone, must equal the plan date.
Late completion on another date does not qualify the original plan date. Reopening a task removes its qualification.
Ordinary note edits do not change `doneAt`.
Legacy `doneAt` values may originate from the v5 backfill. Stats does not invent a more precise completion time.
Missing completion timestamps are flagged and do not qualify a day.

The streak ends at the requested `through` date, or yesterday when the request ends today and today does not yet qualify.
An incomplete today therefore preserves yesterday's streak.
If the consecutive run reaches the requested lower boundary, `isLowerBound` is true.
The view says "At least". A one-day incomplete-today request with no yesterday in range reports the streak as unavailable beyond the range.

Civil date enumeration and timestamp inclusion reuse History's range.
Rolling averages and Monday week keys use Gregorian civil dates independently of local midnight.
DST, a moved midnight, and Apia's skipped date retain the same date attribution as History.
